Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ShortFlow ("we", "us", or "the Service") handles information when people register an account, connect their social-media accounts, use workflows, or test the product. ShortFlow is currently in development.

1. Information we handle

  • Account data: your email address, password hash, and sign-in session information.
  • Connected social accounts: platform identifiers, account names, profile information, authorization tokens and related permissions when you choose to connect Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or Facebook.
  • Video and workflow data: titles, descriptions, videos you provide, published-content metadata, publishing destinations, scheduled jobs and usage records.
  • Subscription records: payment-provider customer identifiers, plan details and subscription status if paid plans are enabled. Payment-card entry is handled by the payment provider rather than ShortFlow.

2. How information is used

We use information to authenticate users, connect social accounts with permission, list and manage videos, prepare or execute requested publishing actions, apply usage limits, provide subscription functionality and diagnose service problems. Social-media authorization is requested for the functions you select, subject to each platform's approved permissions.

3. External platforms and service providers

When you authorize or publish to a connected platform, information is transferred to that provider as needed for the action you request. Those services, including TikTok, Google/YouTube and Meta, have their own privacy policies. If billing is enabled, the payment processor handles payment details under its own privacy notice.

4. Cookies and security

ShortFlow uses a sign-in session cookie to keep you authenticated. This is separate from obtaining social-account access through official OAuth authorization. The application is designed to store social authorization credentials in encrypted form and passwords as hashes. No Internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Retention and account controls

Information is kept as needed to provide the Service, maintain operational records and meet legitimate legal requirements. The application allows connected social accounts to be disconnected and individual video entries to be deleted in supported interfaces. For other access, correction or deletion requests, please contact the operator below. Disconnecting an account does not automatically remove posts already published to third-party platforms.

6. Your choices

You can decide which social accounts to connect and which destinations receive published content. You may revoke ShortFlow's access using the relevant social platform's account settings. Depending on the law where you live, you may have additional rights concerning your personal information.

7. Children

ShortFlow is not designed for children or for people who do not meet the age requirements of the connected platforms.

8. Changes

This policy may be revised as ShortFlow is developed. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
